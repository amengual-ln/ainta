## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept / identity-preserve.
Asset type: A new illustrated source portrait for an animated glyph portrait of Karen Spärck Jones on the Spärck community website. Deliver one single portrait, not a montage or mockup.
Input images: Image 1 is the primary reference for the candid mood, three-quarter head angle, relaxed smile, wind-tousled silver hair, casual posture and clothing. Image 2 is a supporting reference for precise facial identity, age, eyeglasses and anatomy. These are two photographs of the SAME person, Karen Spärck Jones; do not blend into a different or younger person.
Primary request: Create a fresh, respectful artistic interpretation of Karen using both references. Keep her strongly recognizable, naturally elderly, with realistic wrinkles, her characteristic thin metal glasses, silver-white hair, red knit sweater and white collared shirt. Prioritize the warm, lively, informal character of Image 1 over the frontal passport-like composition of Image 2.
Style: refined semi-realistic hand-painted editorial portrait, subtle matte gouache brushwork and natural facial detail, restrained and human; clearly an illustration, not a fabricated historical photograph. No cartoon exaggeration, no anime, no glamor retouching, no porcelain skin.
Composition: vertical 4:5 portrait, chest and shoulders visible, slight shoulder turn and gentle head lean in a natural three-quarter pose. She looks slightly past the viewer toward the left of the image, as if listening to an interesting conversation, with a relaxed open smile. Natural asymmetry. A new composition rather than exactly copying either photograph. Include all of the hair with generous headroom and margins; don't crop the crown or side of the hair. Readable silhouette and distinct facial planes, because the image will later be transformed into glyphs.
Lighting and color: soft natural directional light, enough separation between forehead, glasses, cheek and neck for a readable luminance map; warm natural skin, bone-white hair, rich muted red sweater. Gentle depth, no flat passport lighting, no dramatic colored light.
Background: truly transparent, clean isolated subject, no environment, no props, no shadow outside the silhouette.
Constraints: preserve Karen's identity, age, facial proportions and specific glasses. No text, no watermark, no logos, no mathematical notation, no pre-rendered ASCII, no particles. Output the illustrated portrait itself.
